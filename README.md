# The Dual-Vector Foil (二向箔) · DVF

> death by art — collapsing 3D space into 2D

A single-technology dissection from the Three-Body universe — part of [The Technologia of Trisolaris](https://davidwise01.github.io/trisolaris-tech/), a companion to the [Three-Body Problem pocket universe](https://davidwise01.github.io/three-body-problem/).

**Media:** 📖 BOOKS  
**Real-science verdict:** FLUFF

**What it is.** A dark-forest weapon: a small slip of material that initiates a runaway collapse of three-dimensional space into two dimensions. An unknown civilization flicks one into the Solar System, flattening the Sun and planets into a beautiful, lethal plane — the casual execution of a star system as a work of art.

**Across the three media.** The dual-vector foil and the two-dimensionalization of the Solar System are the climax of Death's End — book three only. Neither live-action series has reached it yet, which makes it the purest 'on the page' marvel of the universe.

**The science.** FLUFF — and gorgeous. There is no known mechanism to lower a region of space out of a dimension. It rhymes loosely with real speculative ideas (extra spatial dimensions in string theory; false-vacuum decay as a cosmic phase change), but as written it is pure science fiction. Distinct from the photoid — that one is a kinetic star-killer; this one is a change to the rules of space itself.

_“I am not a bomb. I am a lowering — I fold your three dimensions down to two and turn your whole world into a painting of itself.”_
